### 5. Enabling Clauses Required in Articles of Association (Form INC-31 e-AOA)
To support our two-tier governance structure, please ensure the Articles of Association include:
1. **Advisory Body Provisions:** Explicit power empowering the Board of Directors to constitute an Academic Advisory Council, appoint advisors, and define their non-statutory terms of reference.
2. **Executive Committees & Non-Director Heads:** Clear provisions authorizing the Board to appoint operational heads, coordinators, and committee leads who do not carry director liabilities.
3. **Director Remuneration for Actual Services:** An enabling clause under Section 8(1) allowing the Foundation to disburse reasonable professional salaries/remuneration to directors for full-time executive and technological services rendered.
