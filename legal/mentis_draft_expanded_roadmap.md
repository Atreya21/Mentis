### III. OBJECTS CLAUSE

#### (A) MAIN OBJECTS:
1. **Advancement of Mathematical Sciences & Foundational Logic:**  
   To promote, advance, encourage, develop, and disseminate the study, teaching, learning, and appreciation of pure and applied mathematics, mathematical logic, statistics, and related theoretical disciplines among students, researchers, educators, and the general public; and to cultivate analytical thinking, problem-solving proficiency, and quantitative reasoning across all tiers of society.
2. **Promotion of Natural & Fundamental Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Biology & Earth Sciences):**  
   To advance, promote, and facilitate education and scientific inquiry in the natural and fundamental sciences—including **physics, chemistry, biological and life sciences, earth and planetary sciences**—emphasizing their mathematical foundations, experimental validation, theoretical modeling, and quantitative formulations.
3. **Interdisciplinary STEM Innovation & Applied Scientific Disciplines:**  
   To foster and promote cross-disciplinary study, research, and applications at the intersection of mathematics, natural sciences, computer science, data science, artificial intelligence, biophysics, chemical physics, quantum information, mathematical biology, computational modeling, and engineering sciences, bridging foundational theory with real-world technological and scientific innovation.
4. **Educational Programmes, Workshops, Seminars, Competitions & Olympiads:**  
   To design, organize, sponsor, and conduct educational initiatives, lecture series, workshops, winter and summer schools, bootcamps, symposia, seminars, conferences, academic exhibitions, and mathematical/scientific competitions or olympiads; and to recognize talent through academic awards, certificates, medals, fellowships, and scholarships to students, educators, and scholars.
5. **Collaborative Research Incubation, Mentorship & Fellowships:**  
   To establish, operate, and maintain an academic and scientific research ecosystem and incubator wherein students, independent scholars, and researchers can formulate, conduct, and collaborate on scientific investigations, connect with experienced academic mentors, form research collectives, and publish or disseminate findings under the flagship and institutional backing of the Foundation.
6. **Digital Infrastructure, Open-Access Platforms & Educational Software:**  
   To conceptualize, develop, operate, and maintain open-access digital platforms, web portals, mobile applications, software tools, knowledge hubs, and interactive community networks (including discussion forums, resource repositories, instructional video channels, reels, and peer-networking platforms) to democratize global access to mathematical, scientific, and educational resources.
7. **Academic Publishing, Scientific Literature & Educational Media:**  
   To author, curate, edit, publish, print, and digitally distribute academic journals, research papers, monographs, newsletters, textbooks, study materials, problem collections, audio-visual educational content, and popular scientific literature in pure mathematics, natural sciences, and interdisciplinary fields.
8. **Institutional Collaborations, Industry Linkages & Global Outreach:**  
   To establish academic linkages, consortia, partnerships, and Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) with schools, colleges, universities, research institutes, scientific academies, government bodies, non-governmental organisations, and corporate entities in India and internationally to further collaborative scientific education and research.

#### (B) ANCILLARY & REVENUE POWERS:
1. **Revenue, Subscriptions & Service Fees:** To collect reasonable admission, event registration, course, and platform service fees.
2. **Grants, Donations & Sponsorships:** To receive voluntary contributions, donations, gifts, sponsorships, and CSR grants.
3. **Intellectual Property Rights:** To apply for, hold, and license trademarks, copyrights, patents, and software rights.
4. **Property & Infrastructure:** To acquire and hold premises, laboratories, and digital server infrastructure.
5. **Banking Operations:** To operate bank accounts and financial instruments.
6. **Remuneration & Staff:** To recruit and pay reasonable remuneration/salaries for actual services rendered.
7. **Prudential Investment:** To invest reserve funds strictly in non-speculative, scheduled instruments.
