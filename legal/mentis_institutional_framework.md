# MENTIS MATHEMATICS FOUNDATION
## INSTITUTIONAL GOVERNANCE & ORGANIZATIONAL FRAMEWORK

---

### 1. FOUNDATIONAL VISION & MISSION

#### Vision
To establish an accessible, borderless mathematics and scientific ecosystem that breaks down institutional barriers to advanced learning, nurtures independent research, and creates an inspiring collaborative network for enthusiasts, students, and scholars worldwide.

#### Mission
* **Democratize Advanced Learning:** Provide open-access curated resources, interactive problem-solving environments, and foundational literature across mathematical and scientific disciplines.
* **Incubate Independent Research:** Empower aspiring scholars and independent thinkers to formulate research investigations, connect with experienced mentors, and publish work under institutional backing.
* **Build Digital Community Infrastructure:** Develop and maintain state-of-the-art interactive platforms connecting students, academicians, and researchers into an active intellectual community.

### 3. TIER I: STATUTORY GOVERNING BOARD

The Governing Board comprises the statutory Directors and Subscribers of the Foundation under the Companies Act, 2013, holding all legal, financial, and regulatory responsibilities:

* **Atreya Ghoshal — Founder & Director**
  * *Jurisdiction:* Overall institutional vision, strategic partnerships, research direction, academic oversight, and legal stewardship.
* **Aritra Ghoshal — Co-Founder & Director**
  * *Jurisdiction:* Core IT infrastructure, digital platform architecture, database security, systems engineering, and technological strategy.
